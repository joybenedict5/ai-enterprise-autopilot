COMPANY HR POLICY DOCUMENT

LEAVE POLICY:
- Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.
- Sick leave: 10 days per year with medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive days.
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave.
- Emergency leave can be approved within 24 hours by HR manager.
- Maternity leave: 16 weeks fully paid.
- Paternity leave: 4 weeks fully paid.

WORKING HOURS:
- Standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM Monday to Friday.
- Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week.
- Overtime must be pre-approved by manager.

ONBOARDING:
- New employees complete 2-week onboarding program.
- Laptop and equipment provided on day 1.
- Buddy system assigned for first 30 days.

EXPENSE POLICY:
- Expenses under $100 are auto-approved.
- Expenses $100-$500 require manager approval.
- Expenses over $500 require director approval.
- All expenses must have receipts.
